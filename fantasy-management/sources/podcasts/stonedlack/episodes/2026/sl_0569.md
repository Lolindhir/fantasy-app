# StonedLack Podcast 569 - ALLE Fantasy Picks NFL Draft 2026

## Overview

The episode is a post-NFL-Draft 2026 fantasy review. StonedLack frames the class as weak for fantasy, especially at running back. Wide receiver depth is more useful than running back depth, but many players are role bets rather than clear alpha bets.

## Main source themes

- Do not rush rookie drafts immediately after the NFL Draft.
- Opportunity and path to the field matter strongly.
- Draft capital buys chances, but role quality still matters.
- Bad landing spots hurt redraft more than dynasty.
- Many day-two tight ends may be real-football fits rather than fantasy receiving assets.

## High-signal players

- Jeremy Love, Cardinals: still likely dynasty 1.01, but weak immediate 2026 environment.
- Price, Seahawks: very strong immediate role signal and redraft appeal.
- Jordan Tyson, Saints: one of the favorite source targets because of a fast path to WR2 usage.
- Casey Concepcion, Browns: should not fall too far due landing spot alone.
- Denzel Boston, Browns: treated as part of a future Browns WR core.
- Chris Bell, Dolphins: strong path-to-WR1 source take.
- Antonio Williams, Commanders: strong day-one WR2 path next to Terry McLaurin.
- Chris Brazzell, Panthers: strong outside WR opportunity.
- Skyler Bell, Bills: positive source profile and good team context.
- Emory Johnson, Chiefs: preferred late RB stash/handcuff type.

## Caution signals

- Many late RBs should not be forced over WRs with clearer paths.
- Tight ends such as Eli Stowers, Max Klare and Marlin Klein need role verification before fantasy upgrades.
- 49ers skill players such as Stribling may have real-football value without fantasy volume.
- Jets pass catchers have investment, but target competition limits enthusiasm.
