## Episode overview

StonedLack reviews every fantasy-relevant 2026 post-draft landing spot by division. The overall source thesis is that the class is weak for fantasy, especially at running back. Wide receiver has more usable darts, but many are role bets rather than clean alpha prospects.

## High-level takeaways

- Do not rush rookie drafts immediately after the NFL Draft.
- Opportunity and path to the field matter more than name value.
- Draft capital buys chances, but role quality still matters.
- Bad landing spots hurt redraft more than dynasty.
- After the first few RBs, StonedLack generally prefers WRs with field paths over low-capital RBs.
- Many day-two TEs may be real-football/blocking fits rather than fantasy receiving assets.
