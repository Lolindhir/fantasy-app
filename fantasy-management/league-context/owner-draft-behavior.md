# Owner Draft Behavior Matrix

Purpose: annually maintained evidence map for predicting how Robert, Marcel, Flo, Jan, Dennis and Tim may behave in Rookie and Free-Agent Drafts.

This file is **not** a deterministic owner-ranking model and does not replace `owner-profiles.md`. It keeps draft-specific evidence in one place so future draft preparation can combine current roster needs with repeated historical behavior.

## Usage rules

- Resolve owner identity through `owner-registry.json`.
- Read current roster, picks and draft pool before applying historical tendencies.
- Keep Rookie Draft and Free-Agent Draft behavior separate.
- Treat direct current target statements and current needs as stronger than older tendencies.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Confidence describes predictive confidence, not whether a past pick fact is certain.
- Append a new annual section after both yearly drafts are complete.
- Do not rewrite old annual evidence because a player later succeeded or failed; outcomes belong in later draft reviews.
- Correct factual source errors when discovered and document the correction.

Evidence levels follow `owner-profiles.md`:

- **Confirmed:** repeated independent evidence or a direct highly diagnostic manager statement.
- **Provisional:** more than one useful signal, but not stable enough for a hard prediction.
- **Candidate:** single or weak signal to watch.
