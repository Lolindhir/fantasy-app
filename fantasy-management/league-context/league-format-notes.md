# League Format Notes

Purpose: stable interpretation notes for the league format.

Canonical current settings still come from current repository data, especially:

- `public/data/League.json`
- `public/data/Metadata.json`

Re-check current data before dynamic roster, salary, pick, standings or trade conclusions.

## Known current structure

Known current format from Fantasy Management rules:

- 6 teams
- 2 QB
- 2 RB
- 2 WR
- 2 TE
- 4 FLEX
- 1 K
- 16 bench spots

## Interpretation rules

- QB is strongly boosted because two fixed QB spots exist.
- TE is strongly boosted because two fixed TE spots exist.
- RB and WR depth matters because four flex spots exist.
- In a 6-team league, replacement level is high.
- Quality and weekly ceiling matter more than mere rosterability.
- Depth still matters because many weekly slots must be filled.
- Kickers are usually replaceable unless current data shows a special reason.

## Player value question

For this league, ask:

Can this player regularly provide meaningful weekly contribution in a 2QB / 2TE / 4Flex format, serve as scarce-position backup, or be used as a trade asset for an upgrade?

## Trade context

In trade analysis, combine format notes with:

- current roster construction
- current picks
- salary and projected salary
- contender / rebuild window
- counterparty roster needs
- owner profile and negotiation history
